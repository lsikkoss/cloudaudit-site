Questions before you book

Something else on your mind? Ask on the call or in the form below.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, bio, text and photos if you have them, plus a few sites you like. The Expert Site includes help structuring and polishing your text. Writing everything from scratch is quoted separately.

What if I don't have testimonials or a logo yet?

No problem. The Results page works with one testimonial or none for now, and we show you how to collect more. A logo can be quoted separately.

Who owns the website?

You do: the design, content, files and domain. We hand everything over at launch.

Do you need my passwords?

No. Where we need access to your domain or hosting, you add us as a collaborator and can remove us at any time.

Can you build something more complex, like a portal or store?

Yes. That's the Custom Build. We scope it on a call and send a fixed quote with its own schedule before any work starts.

Do you work with clients outside Minnesota?

Yes. Everything happens online: calls, previews and reviews, in English or French.
