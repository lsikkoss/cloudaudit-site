Access

What we can see, and what we can't

Access is read-only and limited to cost and resource metadata. Nothing in your account can be changed through it.

What the audit uses

  • Billing and Cost Explorer data
  • Lists of resources: servers, databases, volumes, load balancers, buckets
  • Usage metrics such as CPU and network traffic
  • Savings Plan and reserved capacity status

What we never need

  • Your application code or customer data
  • Database contents or the files inside your buckets
  • Passwords, keys and secrets
  • Root credentials, IAM users or access keys
  • Permission to create, change or delete anything
How access works

You stay in control at every step

1

You review the permissions

Read the exact policy below, or have your engineer check it, before you create anything.

2

You create the role

One role in your own account, from our CloudFormation template or by hand. We never create anything there.

3

We run the audit

We analyze your cost and usage data and write up each finding with its savings, effort and risk.

4

Access ends at delivery

When your report is delivered you delete the role, and we confirm in writing that we no longer use it. You can revoke it yourself at any moment.

AWS CloudTrail records each use of the role in your account, so you can see exactly when we accessed it.

Cost and billing data

Spend by service, account and tag, budgets, Savings Plan status and AWS list prices. It shows where the money goes.

Recommendations

AWS's own rightsizing and savings suggestions, so we can cross-check our findings against theirs.

Compute and network inventory

Lists of servers, volumes, snapshots, NAT gateways and load balancers, to find idle and oversized resources. No startup scripts.

Database inventory

Type, size and snapshots of databases, never their contents.

Storage inventory

Bucket names, regions, lifecycle rules and tags, never the files.

Usage metrics

CPU, network and request counts, to see what is actually used.

Explicit deny

Blocks reading S3 objects, secrets, parameters, KMS decryption, DynamoDB items, queue messages and Windows passwords, whatever else is attached later.

Questions

Straight answers before you share any access

Can you change anything in my AWS account?

No. The role has no write permissions, so we can look but not create, modify or delete anything. Any changes are made by your team.

Will you see my customers' data?

No. The role cannot read the contents of storage buckets or databases, and an explicit deny blocks it. We see how much is stored and what it costs, not what is in it.

Who works on my audit?

Kossi Sikpe leads every audit and runs your walkthrough call. First-pass analysis may involve freelance cloud specialists. We share only what a job needs, never one client's data with another, and every finding is checked before it reaches you.

What if the audit finds very little?

That is what the guarantee is for. If we don't identify at least twice our fee in annual savings, you get a full refund.

Do you implement the changes?

The audit delivers a ranked report and a roadmap. Hands-on help afterward is a separate Implementation project with its own scoped access that you approve.
