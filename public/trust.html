What we need, and what we never need

Most of the work uses only the content you give us. When we need access to your accounts, you stay in control.

What we need

  • Your logo, text and photos (or we help you shape them)
  • Examples of sites you like
  • Collaborator access to your domain or hosting, only if needed
  • Your calendar and email tool links for booking and sign-ups

What we never need

  • Your passwords
  • Your bank or card details (payments go through your payment provider)
  • Ownership of your domain or accounts
  • Anything you haven't chosen to share

You stay in control at every step

  1. You keep your accounts

    Your domain and hosting stay in your name, registered by you.

  2. You add us as a collaborator

    Where access is needed, you invite us as a collaborator or delegate. We never ask for your password.

  3. We build and show you

    You see a design preview first, then a working draft of the site before anything goes live.

  4. Access ends at handoff

    When we hand over your site, you remove our access, and we confirm in writing that we no longer use it. You can also remove us at any moment.

Common questions

Who works on my site?

I lead every project and review everything before you see it. Independent specialists may help with parts of the work; they only receive what the job needs, and they never get your passwords.

Do I need to give you my passwords?

No. Where access is needed, you add us as a collaborator and can remove us at any time.

What if I don't like the design?

Tell us within 5 business days of the first design preview and we refund your deposit. After that, two rounds of changes are included.

Who owns the website?

You do: design, content, files and domain, handed over at launch.

Do you show previous work?

Our portfolio is growing. The home page shows concept designs, and on a call we'll tell you honestly what else we can show. That's why the deposit-back guarantee exists.
